## Slide 1: iPad Pro, One Name, Many Models

- Apple's most powerful tablet line
- Made by Apple since 2015
- iPadOS 27 arrived September 14, 2026

![The backs of two iPad Pro models with M5, one space black and one silver, showing the rear cameras.](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/images/2025/10/apple-introduces-the-powerful-new-ipad-pro-with-the-m5-chip/article/Apple-iPad-Pro-color-lineups-251015_inline.jpg.large.jpg)

### Speaker Notes

The first-ever iPad Pro was released in 2015. It had the A9X chip and ran iOS 9.1.

---

## Slide 2: 12.9-inch iPad Pro, Sixth Generation

- Released October 2022
- M2 chip, mini-LED display
- Apple Pencil hover

![The 2022 iPad Pro in 12.9-inch and 11-inch sizes side by side, with colorful wallpaper on the screens.](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/images/product/ipad/standard/Apple-iPad-Pro-hero-2up-221018_big.jpg.large.jpg)

### Speaker Notes

The 12.9-inch iPad Pro, sixth generation, is perfect for creators. It can handle demanding tasks like running digital audio workstations (DAWs) and video editing.

---

## Slide 3: 13-inch iPad Pro with M4

- Released May 2024
- Tandem OLED display
- Only 5.1 millimeters thin

![The 13-inch iPad Pro with M4 seen edge-on from the side. It looks like a thin line.](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/images/2024/05/apple-unveils-stunning-new-ipad-pro-with-m4-chip-and-apple-pencil-pro/article/Apple-iPad-Pro-thin-profile-240507_big.jpg.large.jpg)

### Speaker Notes

The iPad Pro with M4 is perfect for extremely demanding tasks like 3D rendering. These powerhouses can do many things that a Mac computer can.

---

## Slide 4: 13-inch iPad Pro with M5

- Released October 2025
- Much faster AI
- Wi-Fi 7, faster charging

![The iPad Pro with M5 attached to a Magic Keyboard, with several apps open on the screen.](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/images/2025/10/apple-introduces-the-powerful-new-ipad-pro-with-the-m5-chip/article/Apple-iPad-Pro-hero-251015_big.jpg.large.jpg)

### Speaker Notes

This iPad is perfect for on-device AI workflows, like running AI models on the device itself. This beast performs very well on complex tasks.

---

## Slide 5: Why It Matters

- Students, artists, and video editors
- Benefit: laptop power, tablet size
- Next model rumored for 2027

![A hand drawing on the iPad Pro with M5 using an Apple Pencil Pro.](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/images/2025/10/apple-introduces-the-powerful-new-ipad-pro-with-the-m5-chip/article/Apple-iPad-Pro-Apple-Pencil-Pro-251015_big.jpg.large.jpg)

Source: [MacRumors, September 2026](https://www.macrumors.com/roundup/ipados-27/)

### Speaker Notes

iPads in general are useful for artists, video editors, and music producers. These devices will keep getting more powerful with every software and hardware update.
